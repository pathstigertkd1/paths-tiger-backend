Frequently Asked Questions

🥋 What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable workout clothes like t-shirts and sweatpants are perfect for your first few classes. We'll help you get fitted for a uniform when you're ready.

🤔 Do I need any experience?

Not at all! We welcome beginners of all ages. Our programs are designed to take you from white belt to black belt at your own pace.

📅 How do I book a trial class?

Simply fill out the contact form or give us a call. We'll schedule a free trial class at a time that works for you.

💰 What are your membership fees?

We offer flexible membership plans starting from Rs. 7,900/month. Check our Schedule page for details.
