Grand Master Malik Shahid

4th Degree Black Belt • 14+ Years Experience

Grand Master Malik Shahid began his Taekwondo journey at age 15 in Lahore Pakistan. He has trained International athletes, coached national champions, and most importantly, guided thousands of children and adults in their personal martial arts journey.

His teaching philosophy centers on the belief that Taekwondo is not just about physical techniques, but about building character, discipline, and respect for oneself and others.

📖 Our Story

Founded in 2012, Paths Tiger Taekwondo Academy started in a small community center with just 15 students. Today, we're proud to be one of the most respected martial arts schools in the region.

The name "Paths Tiger" represents our belief that every student walks their own unique path, and we help them unleash their inner tiger—courageous, focused, and strong.
